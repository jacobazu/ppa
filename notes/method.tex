{\sffamily\bfseries\Large PPA pricing in four areas: methods}\par
\href{https://github.com/jacobazu/ppa}{github.com/jacobazu/ppa} \textperiodcentered{} October 2026

The fair fixed price and the risk of a 10-year PPA (2027--2036) for a solar park and an onshore wind farm in DK1, SE3, Finland and Germany, each producing 100\,GWh in an average weather year. A case study in Python.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lL@{}}
\toprule
Contracts & Pay-as-produced: the buyer takes all output at a fixed price. Baseload: the park delivers its average output every hour and buys any shortfall at the day-ahead price. \\
Capture rate & Output-weighted price over the average price, per area and year \citep{hirth2013}. The park stops when the price is negative. \\
Price level & Exchange forward prices for each year, the last listed year held flat (the last 12 months' average where there are none); for DK1 also Energistyrelsen's outlook \citep{soeb25}. The yearly level moves as an AR(1) in logs \citep{lucia2002}, estimated on DK1's yearly prices 2000--2025 and scaled to keep the expected level. \\
Hourly patterns & Prices and production of one of the last three 12-month periods, one drawn for each contract year, so the link between when a park produces and what power is worth is kept. \\
Volume & 35 weather years (1991--2025) from ERA5 \citep{hersbach2020}: hourly wind at 100\,m through a Vestas V126 power curve \citep{staffell2016}, and yearly solar radiation. \\
Fair price & The production-weighted expected price \citep{qorbanian2024}: both sides get the same expected present value, at 5.6\% (3.5\% real, \citealp{fm2026}, plus 2\% inflation). Split into the forward price, the park's profile and the price--volume covariance \citep{chatziandreou2026}. No risk premium. \\
Risk & 20{,}000 paths. The 1 in 20 worst 10-year present value and the average below it \citep{artzner1999}, for the park and the buyer. \\
Hedge & The baseload volume sold forward that minimises the variance of the 10-year income \citep{ederington1979}. \\
Sensitivities & Only the last 12 months' pattern; no stop at negative prices; capture rates one point lower each year. \\
Rules & Assumptions are set before running and listed with sources in the README. Not included: balancing costs, tariffs, subsidies, counterparty risk, and a link between windy years and low prices. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
